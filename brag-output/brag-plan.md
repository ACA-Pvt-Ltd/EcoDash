# Brag Plan: EcoDash

## What is this app?
A three-sided recycling marketplace (mobile app + admin portal): households list their waste, collectors buy it and pay out points or cash, and vendors buy the sorted waste from collectors.

## The angle
"Your waste has a price." EcoDash's own tagline is *Turn Waste into Worth*, so the video follows one bag of plastic through all three roles. It starts as your trash, becomes points and rupees, and ends up as a vendor's stock. Each role gets one screen from the real app, doing its real job.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Dark forest-green frame. "Your waste" fades up, then "has a price." slams in on the first beat. It reads as a claim, not a slogan.

## Key moments (the middle)
- **User, Create Waste Offer:** a finger taps the *Plastic* chip, "12" types into the weight field, the estimate shows *120 pts · LKR 60*, and *Create Offer* gets tapped.
- **Collector, Scan User QR Code:** a scan line sweeps a QR code, *Verify User* turns green, and *Points to Award* counts 0 → 120.
- **Vendor, Vendor Dashboard:** three stat cards arrive one by one: *Today Weight*, *Today Purchases*, *Total Spent*.

## Outro / punchline
The chain You → Collector → Vendor snaps together, then the EcoDash wordmark and the logo in rings appear with "Turn Waste into Worth".

## User flow worth showing
Entry: the user lists 12 kg of plastic → Key action: the collector scans the user's QR code and awards points → Result: the vendor dashboard fills with purchased stock.

## Tone
- Preset: default
- Creative direction: bright, confident, community-proud launch spot
- Interpretation: clean crossfades, readable holds, playful but not jokey. The pride comes from showing that a real three-role flow works.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 21.5s

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: gradient #2DD36F → #1FAF5B → #16874A (welcome screen); app surfaces #F5F6FA / #E8F5E9
- Accent: #2ECC71 (buttons), #16874A (deep green text on white)
- Text: #FFFFFF on the gradient, #11181C on app surfaces
- Display font: platform system font, weight 800, tight tracking (as in `appName`)
- Body font: platform system font
- Strongest visual element: the welcome screen's logo inside three translucent rings, with the tagline between two thin divider lines

## Share copy (draft)
Your waste has a price. EcoDash links households, collectors and vendors so 12 kg of plastic turns into 120 points, a pickup and a vendor's stock.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm bed
- Music: happy-beats-business-moves-vol-9 (114.84 BPM)
- Music treatment: starts at 0, volume ~0.5, 0.3s fade-in, 1.5s fade-out under the final logo
- Music cue guidance: preset read. Strong cues: 3.70s (EcoDash wordmark), 6.34s (cut to the user scene), 10.54s (cut to the collector scene). Beat-grid window for the vendor cards: 15.28 / 15.81 / 16.34, with the full set held until 17.91.
- Audio-reactive treatment: none (restraint; the product UI is the visual)
- SFX posture: sparse, motion-matched
- Audio-coupled moments: hook slam, logo bell, chip tap, weight typing, button tap and toast, verify chime, card slides, outro bell
- Restraint rule: no stacked hits; nothing over the music on the text holds

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Hook — 2.65s (0.00–2.65)
Dark green. "Your waste" fades up, then "has a price." slams in at 1.07.
Sequential/interaction: two lines, one after the other
Audio intent: soft impact on the slam
Transition mood: clean → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — Reveal — 3.69s (2.65–6.34)
Welcome-screen gradient. "WELCOME TO", then "EcoDash" lands at 3.70 (beat-locked), then the tagline "Turn Waste into Worth" with dividers. The logo inside its rings scales in on the right.
Audio intent: bell on the wordmark
Transition mood: clean → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — You list it — 4.20s (6.34–10.54)
Mint background. Caption "1 · You list it" on the left. A phone shows *Create Waste Offer*: waste-type chips. *Plastic* is tapped (7.40), "12" types into *Weight (kg)*, the estimate reads *120 pts · LKR 60* (8.96), *Create Offer* is tapped (9.50), and the toast "Offer posted" appears.
Sequential/interaction: yes. Simulated tap, typing, button press.
Audio intent: click, key ticks, click, bong
Transition mood: clean → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — A collector picks it up — 4.22s (10.54–14.76)
Caption "2 · A collector picks it up". The phone shows *Scan User QR Code*: the scan line sweeps, *Verify User* turns to a check (12.12), *Points to Award* counts to 120 and *Cash to Award* reads LKR 60 (count finishes at 12.65).
Sequential/interaction: yes. Scan, verify, count-up.
Audio intent: bong on verify
Transition mood: clean → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — A vendor buys it — 3.15s (14.76–17.91)
Caption "3 · A vendor buys it". The phone shows *Vendor Dashboard*. Stat cards *Today Weight 340 kg*, *Today Purchases 8* and *Total Spent LKR 4,200* arrive at 15.28 / 15.81 / 16.34, then the full set holds.
Audio intent: soft card slides
Transition mood: clean → Scene 6

### Scene 6 — Outro — 3.59s (17.91–21.50)
Gradient. The chips You → Collector → Vendor snap in (17.91), then the EcoDash wordmark, the ringed logo and "Turn Waste into Worth" (18.96). Hold. The music fades out.
Audio intent: soft hit, then a bell

**Music mood for this video:** upbeat
**Audio summary:** A warm business-beats bed carries the whole video. Sparse UI clicks follow each simulated tap, and bells mark the two appearances of the brand.

## Data note
All numbers on screen are illustrative. The rates (Plastic: 10 pts/kg, LKR 5/kg) are the app's real defaults from `frontend/constants/config.ts`. No real user names, emails, URLs, API hosts or keys appear.
